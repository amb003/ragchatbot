# New Hire Laptop Onboarding

Corporate HR process for **employee laptops** at headquarters — not for retail POS or store networks.

## Steps

1. Create Entra ID account.
2. Ship laptop from depot.
3. Assign O365 license.

**Not applicable** to store POS offline tickets.
